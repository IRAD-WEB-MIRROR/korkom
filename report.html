The year

The Palace of Art opened the Saparbekov horses room and conserved 38 icons. Hunarmand: 22 masters, 41 apprentices, the first winter fair. Children's studios opened in Kyzyl-Adyr and Toktogul. Ak-Kuu toured eight district towns. Zara Toktarova joined the Board of Trustees.

Millions of som, rounded. Audited by Talas Audit Consulting LLC (opinion of 28.04.2026). The full report (PDF, 4.2 MB) is available from the office on request.
