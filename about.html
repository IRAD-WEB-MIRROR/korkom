About Us

The Körköm Foundation was registered on 14 March 2011 by IradOne Media Group, Talasbank, Attelkom and a circle of Talas patrons. Körköm means art; the Palace of Art in Iyık Janyl is Irad Körköm Saraiy. In 2010 its roof leaked over the Ayyubov Hall, and the money to mend it came from five donors in one week. The foundation was set up so that the next roof would not depend on luck.

We are a private non-profit foundation, not a state body. We fund museums, ensembles, crafts masters, young artists and children's art studios, and we give our members a home on the web. Part of our income is a subsidy from the Ministry of Education under its programme "Culture and the Arts"; the rest comes from companies, patrons and the Winter Gala (see the annual report).
